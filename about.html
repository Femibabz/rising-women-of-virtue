About the Founder

Guided by Faith, Purpose, and Heritage

"To whom much is given, much is expected."

The founder of Women on the Rise is a woman of deep faith and unwavering purpose, guided by the belief that "to whom much is given, much is expected." Rooted in her African heritage and grounded in gratitude for the opportunities she has embraced, she carries a profound sense of humility and responsibility in every space she enters.

For decades, she cultivated her gift for supporting and uplifting others while working in higher education across multiple colleges and universities. Although she found fulfillment in guiding students outside the traditional classroom, she felt a calling to do more, to expand her impact, to reach further, and to serve women in a deeper, more intentional way.

Today, she is a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, a role that allows her to use her voice, compassion, and insight to guide individuals, couples, and families through life challenges. She is known for her genuine presence, her calm wisdom, and her ability to be a grounding point for those navigating emotional and mental struggles. She considers this work not just a profession, but her life's purpose that cultivates deeper meaning to her existence.

Organizational Philosophy & Purpose

Supporting the Whole Person

We believe that every woman deserves the freedom to express her authentic truth, explore her vulnerabilities without fear of judgment, and step confidently into her personal power with dignity, self-worth, and autonomy. Our organization is committed to creating a safe, welcoming, and supportive environment that fosters hope, healing, personal growth, and transformation.

Rising Women of Virtue recognizes that women often face a variety of emotional, mental, social, and personal challenges that can hinder their ability to reach their full potential. To effectively support women on their journey toward self-discovery and empowerment, our programs are designed to address the whole person through a holistic and strengths-based approach. Special attention is given to emotional wellness, mental well-being, social connectedness, psychoeducation, and personal development.

Through compassionate guidance, meaningful support, and evidence-based educational opportunities, Rising Women of Virtue is dedicated to helping women navigate life’s struggles and uncertainties while building the confidence, resilience, and skills necessary to thrive. Our goal is to empower women to move beyond survival and toward lives characterized by purpose, stability, self-confidence, and fulfillment.

Compassionate Guidance

A Safe Space for Transformation

Her clinical approach is rooted in warmth, authenticity, and a steadfast commitment to creating a safe, non-judgmental space.

She believes every woman deserves the freedom to express her truth, explore her vulnerabilities, and step into her power with dignity and autonomy. Her greatest strengths lie in her ability to connect with sincerity, hold space with compassion, and honor the unique story each individual carries.

Driven by a passion to make a meaningful difference, she empowers women to rediscover their inner strengths, embrace their purpose, and pursue a life aligned with their values, healing, and aspirations without fear. Through Rising Women of Virtue, she continues her mission to help women stand tall, rise strong, and walk confidently into the fullness of who they are meant to be.

Our Core Commitment

  • Trauma-Informed Care: Low-barrier spaces honoring individual lived experiences and emotional safety.
  • Strengths-Based Approach: Focusing on built-in resilience, personal autonomy, and authentic growth.
  • Inclusive Community: Welcoming women ages 18–65 facing life transitions, burnout, or socioeconomic hurdles.
  • Faith & Purpose: Grounded in the foundational belief that every woman possesses inherent dignity and virtue.
