Non-Profit FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our non-profit group meetings, participation, and organization structure.

Are there any fees to participate in Rising Women of Virtue programs?

No. Rising Women of Virtue is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing accessible, low-barrier support. All peer encouragement groups, life skills education, and restoration activities are free of cost for participants.

Who can join Rising Women of Virtue?

We support women (ages 18–65) who are facing critical life challenges, major transitions, burnout, emotional hurdles, or socioeconomic hardship. Our programs welcome women seeking personal growth, community connection, and purpose-driven living.

What can I expect during a support group session?

Our empowerment circles provide encouragement, meaningful connection, and a safe, confidential environment for reflection and growth. Groups are facilitated with warmth, compassion, and a strengths-based focus on emotional wellness.

How can I volunteer or support the organization?

We would appreciate donations towards the work we are doing. Such donations can be sent through Zelle to support@risingwomenofvirtue.org. If you will prefer other forms of pledge, kindly fill the form above.
